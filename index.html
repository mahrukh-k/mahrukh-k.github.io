A bit about how I work

I'm a data scientist working at the intersection of economics, geospatial analysis, and public policy. Over the past several years I've worked with institutions including the World Bank Group, the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, and the Center for Economic Research in Pakistan — building pipelines that fuse satellite data, administrative records, and survey data into research that's ready for decision-makers, not just journals.

I care about the last mile: turning a model or a dataset into something a policy team can actually act on — a dashboard they check weekly, a brief that changes a resource allocation, a repository the next analyst can pick up without guessing.
